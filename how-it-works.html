HOW THE LEDGER WORKS

A simple 6-step protocol linking physical craftsmanship to an ephemeral digital voice.

01

TAP NFC / SCAN QR

Locate the wash-tested NFC tag embedded inside your garment. Tap with your smartphone or scan the backup code to instantly open your garment's terminal page.

02

REVEAL CLAIM CARD

Unbox your package and find the sealed physical Claim Card. Scratch off the protective layer to reveal your one-time secret access key.

03

CLAIM YOUR PERMANENT NUMBER

Enter the scratch key on your garment's terminal page. The key burns permanently, forever linking your account to that garment's unique number (#0001 - #0777).

04

CHOOSE A SIGNAL COLOR

Select your statement color for the cycle: Red, Green, Blue, or Black. The garment is a blank canvas — you decide the color identity.

05

PUBLISH FOR EXACTLY 7 DAYS

Write an anonymous statement up to 140 characters. Once published, you have a 7-minute grace window to edit typos before it locks for 7 full days.

06

RETURN AFTER THE CYCLE

After 168 hours (7 days), your statement automatically vanishes from public view. The garment returns to silence, ready for your next expression.

SYSTEM PROTOCOL RULES

  • Private Preview: Unsaved typing simulations on the homepage are strictly local and visible only to you.
  • One Permanent Number: Each garment carries a single permanent number assigned during quality control.
  • 7-Minute Grace Window: You can fix typos within 7 minutes of publishing. After 7 minutes, the statement locks completely.
  • Cooldown Period: Hiding a statement early does not reset your 7-day cooldown timer.
  • Multiple Cycles: A single garment can experience infinite 7-day cycles over its lifetime.
